LEADERSHIP & HERITAGE

Led by Mr. Vilas Shinde

Founded in 1999 in Nashik, Maharashtra, SQF is led by mechanical engineer and lead auditor Mr. Vilas Shinde. With nearly three decades of practical field experience in quality, safety, security, and sustainability, SQF bridges the gap between standard clauses and shop-floor reality.

Our work focuses on building self-sustaining internal capability so teams stay audit-ready and continuously improve long after initial engagement.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Why organizations choose SQF

  • Practical systems: Designed around how your operations actually run, avoiding shelf-ware documentation.
  • 100% Independent & impartial: Clear separation between consultancy/training and third-party certification bodies.
  • Multi-sector expertise: Deep domain coverage across manufacturing, automotive, chemicals, food, agrovet, and infrastructure.
  • End-to-end partnership: From initial gap assessment to internal auditor training and final readiness review.
